In memory of 1971

National Martyrs' Memorial

জাতীয় স্মৃতিসৌধ

Syed Mainul Hossain's seven pairs of triangular walls rise to a 150 foot peak, one for each of the seven struggles from 1952 to 1971.

21 February 1952

Central Shaheed Minar

কেন্দ্রীয় শহীদ মিনার

Built for the students who gave their lives for the Bangla language. Hamidur Rahman and Novera Ahmed shaped it as a mother with her martyred sons, under a red rising sun.

Louis Kahn, 1982

National Parliament House

জাতীয় সংসদ ভবন

Louis Kahn's concrete masterpiece in Dhaka. Giant circles and triangles cut into the walls let the daylight in, and a lake wraps around it so it seems to float.

National animal

Royal Bengal Tiger

রয়েল বেঙ্গল টাইগার

The pride of the Sundarbans, and the only tigers on Earth that live in a mangrove forest. In 2024 the survey counted 125 of them on our side of the forest.

Folk art on three wheels

The Rickshaw

রিকশা

Every hood, seat and panel is painted by hand. Dhaka is called the rickshaw capital of the world, and its rickshaw art is on the UNESCO heritage list.

UNESCO World Heritage

Sixty Dome Mosque

ষাট গম্বুজ মসজিদ

Built in the 15th century by Khan Jahan Ali in Bagerhat. The name says sixty, but the roof carries 77 domes. This is a 3D scan of the real building.
